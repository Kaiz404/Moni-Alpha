# @repo/core

Pure domain rules the phone app and the web app share. Raw TypeScript, no build step: Metro reads it directly, and Next.js through `transpilePackages`.

| Module | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| `ai-consent.ts` | The user's choice about Moni's AI from the `ai_consent_current` rows (`consentFromRows`), whether a use may run (`aiUseAllowed`), and what Turn on and Not now record. |
| `backup/` | Export data and Import data. `moni-export.ts` is the Moni export format: `TABLES` (the one list of columns), `TABLE_ORDER`, `buildMoniExport`, `exportFileName`, and `backupTables`, what an export holds of the store's rows. `csv.ts` reads and writes the cells, `decode.ts` the bytes (UTF-8, UTF-16, Windows-1252), `read-moni-export.ts` reads an export back, and `generic-csv.ts` reads any other CSV (`guessMapping`, `guessDateOrder`, `readImportRows`). `import-file.ts` sorts a chosen file into a restore, a CSV to map, or a reason (`readImportFile`), and `planImport` plans it; `plan.ts` holds the idempotence rule (`planRestore`, `planGeneric`, `restoreTotals`), and `preview.ts`'s `previewRows` shows the first rows of a CSV beside how Moni reads each (day label, merchant after merchant names, signed amount) and, once a wallet is chosen, whether it is new or already here, from `planGeneric`. Dates read in the `Calendar` passed in. |
| `calendar.ts` | `createCalendar(zone)` reads an instant as a day key, a month key or a day number in one IANA zone, and finds when a day starts or when its clock reads a time (`wallTime`). Day and month arithmetic (`addDays`, `daysBetween`, `daysInMonth`, `shiftMonth`, `addMonths`) needs no zone. |
| `glossary.ts` | The words `docs/redesign/glossary.md` fixes: money (`RM1,116.20`, U+2212, whole ringgit for forward-looking numbers), day labels, due phrases, month names and chart ticks. Every date word takes day keys. |
| `ledger.ts` | What a row means: whether a transaction counts as spending, as income or as cash flow, whether it is filed, and what it does to a wallet's balance; a debt's totals, its stored status, and its state on a day (open, due soon, overdue, settled, written off). |
| `receipts.ts` | `receiptPath`: where a stored receipt photo sits in the `receipts` bucket, from a capture's `source_image_uri` or a transaction's `receipt_image_url` (a path, or the path inside a public URL stored before #72), or null while the photo is still on the phone. Approve and pairing move a photo to the ledger row only once it has a path. |
| `capture/` | What Moni makes of a capture: routine detection (`matchRoutine`), the occurred-at date, Type it's draft (`draftFromExtraction`), and the category guess (`guessCategory` over `captureClues`): the prefill a capture gets and the category sheet's up to three Suggested tiles, from the user's filing, `CATEGORY_CLUES` and the reader's word. |
| `chart-range.ts` | The chart ranges (1M, 7D, 30D, 90D, 1Y, All) and where each starts. |
| `money-month/` | One user's month of money: `moneyLedger` reads the synced rows once (the user's categories, budgets, monthly total and this month's bills from `detectRecurring` and the overrides), and `buildMoneyMonth` answers any month: spend per day, even pace and the days at or under it (`evenPace`, one rounding), bills and debts due, what is left and safe to spend a day (`safePerDay`), per-category spend, and the sentences each screen shows (`words.ts`). `nextDays` lists the bills and debts due in Home's next 14 days. Bills, recurring series and the calendar heat live here too, and `spendByMonth` with `lastMonthAndAverage`: each category's spending over the months before one, for Budget's last month and 3-month average columns and its category history, and `suggestionMonths`, the history Ask Moni suggests a budget from. |
| `write-plans/` | Ledger commands as plans: each returns the rows to insert, patch and soft-delete (`RowWrites`), or why nothing can be written (`needsInput`, `error`), and writes nothing itself. Transactions and bulk edits, the one transfer rule (`checkTransfer`), balance correction, category budgets (`planCategoryBudget`, which revives a soft-deleted row since the table key keeps it, and `budgetsFromSpending`), a new category last in the user's list with no active twin (`planCategoryCreate`), debts with their cash mirrors and status (and `planDebtEdit` for its own fields, `planDebtDelete` for a whole debt), wallet deletion with its transactions, merchant names for many codes at once (`planMerchantNames`, which revives a forgotten name's row, and `planForgetMerchantNames`), Add a bill (`planAddBill`, a new bill or one made from a payment under its series, reviving the series' row), and import rows without gated columns. Plans read a `LedgerView` (the read model's live wallets, transactions, debts and activities) and a `PlanContext` (user, now, new ids, which gated columns exist). `applyToTables` is the in-memory applier. Review decisions (`review.ts`): `planReview` takes a capture, a decision (Approve, with the user's edits, "Don't ask about similar", Moni's own Auto mark and where the phone was, or Not mine) and the wallets and auto-add rules, and returns the ledger row, the closed capture, the rule, and the `CaptureMark`s for the phone's capture log, which the web ignores; `planReopen` is a decision's Undo, `planSimilarRule` the rule on its own, `planNotATransfer`, `planCaptureWallet` and `retypeEdits` fix a capture before it is decided, and `editedProposal`, how familiar a merchant is, the captures Approve all usual takes and the bank alert a notification capture keeps (`captureAlert`, `alertKey`) live beside them; the check-in's answer (`planCheckIn`), Add a place (`planAddPlace`, which pins expenses to one of the user's places), and `undoWrites`, the writes that put a plan's rows back for a toast's Undo. |
| `chat/` | What Moni chat sends and shows. `buildChatSnapshot` turns the projection, the user, the recurring verdicts and the question into the `finance_assistant_tool_v4` snapshot `POST /v1/chat/analyze` takes: per-currency metrics (`finance-metrics.ts` over `insight-metrics.ts` and `budget-coach-metrics.ts`) and facts (`facts.ts`: balances, recurring series, payday, `parseAskedAmount`; `forecast.ts`: the forecast, the afford check and `backtestForecast`). `walletsNeedingConfirmation` (`confirmation.ts`) names the balances Home's check-in asks about. `buildAnswerSnapshot` reads this month in the main currency and `pickAnswerCard` picks and fills the answer card a question asks for; `resolveRoute` (`routing.ts`) tells a question from a capture without the model. `budgetReply` (`budget-ask.ts`) steps Suggest a budget through chat: a pick names a budget, each reply answers the next question, and the last one asks for the amount. For the web: `draftCategoryChange` (`draft.ts`) reads "Move my Grab rides out of Food" or "put these in Transport" against the rows in view and drafts the category change (the rows, from and to, the total; with only "out of" named, the target is where that merchant's other payments sit), with `draftIntro` and `draftAfter` (each category's month once approved); `spendByWeek` and `pickViewAnswerCard` (`week-card.ts`) lay a category's month out by week against the even pace and give a spending question the view's category. `pickAnswerCard` is unchanged for the phone. |
| `charts/` | `month-charts.ts`: the web's month charts laid out for any box: Pace and Daily (`paceChart`, `dailyChart`), Activity's running totals against last month (`runningChart`), the 1M ticks with Today, and `nearestDay` for a pointer. `ring.ts`: the category ring both apps draw (`ringSegments`, `ringHit`) and the words its middle reads at rest (`spentCentre`: the total, then "RM116 over", "of RM1,800" or "spent"). |
| `read-model/` | The finance read model: synced rows as finance types (`rows.ts`), the projection with transactions by wallet and activities by debt (`projectFinance`), and what the screens read from it: wallet balances, net worth, budget progress, category spend (`selections.ts`), merchants, places (`places.ts`: grouping, ranges, the wallet filter, the expenses without a place, the days of a range and a place's spending by day), the map maths both maps share (`place-map.ts`: `clusterPins` merges pins that touch, `fitProjector` lays coordinates into a box for the web's drawn map, `regionFor` and `projector` for the phone's), Activity's filter and chart series, a See all list's scope (`list-scope.ts`), spend and balance history, the Debts summary and the month report. Review's inbox (`review-inbox.ts`): the oldest capture first (`inboxOrder`), the source pill (`reviewStatus`), Recently reviewed (`reviewedLately`, 30 days), the "Don't ask about similar" rules listed beside it (`auto-add-rules.ts`: `liveAutoAddRules`, `autoAddRuleItems`), and for the open capture the alerts "Don't ask about similar" would have matched this month (`similarMatches`) and its merchant's history (`merchantHistory`). A transaction's row (`transactionRow`, `compactWalletNames`), the check-in's wallet (`walletToCheckIn`), and the recurring verdicts and merchant names from their synced rows (`user-rows.ts`). |

The zone is always a parameter. Mobile builds its calendar from the device zone (`apps/mobile/lib/dates/device-calendar.ts`); the web will build it from the profile zone.

```bash
pnpm --filter @repo/core test         # Vitest
pnpm --filter @repo/core check-types
pnpm --filter @repo/core lint
```
