# Web app: implementation handoff

Moni is getting a full web app with the same features as the phone app, plus desktop-only capabilities. The design is locked on the claude.ai design canvas. This file tells an implementing agent where the design lives, which boards are final, the rules every screen follows, and what is still the owner's call. The architecture and the build order are in [`web/PLAN.md`](web/PLAN.md). The prompt for an implementing agent is in [`web/PROMPT.md`](web/PROMPT.md).

## Layouts

- Width 1360 and up: the full Ink sidebar (W2-Parts still says 1280; W2-Responsive's 1360 wins). Its fold button (or `[`) folds it to the rail and back, remembered per browser; narrower windows always show the rail. 1024 to 1359: the labelled rail. 700 to 1023: the rail with one column.
- Under 700 wide, or under 500 tall: the **phone layout**, which is the mobile v2 app's design drawn in the same Next.js app (floating Ink tab bar, the same cards and words). It is not a third design and not a separate app.
- The layout follows the viewport, not the device. Crossing a breakpoint keeps the route, the selected row and any half-typed field, because that state lives in the URL and the store.
- iPad and other touch screens keep the desktop layout, with hover-only affordances made visible (⋯ on the selected row, long-press as right-click) and targets of at least 44.

## Rules that apply to every web screen

- All of `REDESIGN.md`'s rules, with the exceptions below. One main currency on focused pages, one progress style, charts lead, one home per chart, visible affordances, Approve and Not mine, Delete visible on a detail view, numbered pages, every state change animates.
- **The sidebar and the rail list Home, Activity, Budget, Debts, Wallets and Map**, one word each (#144), then Ask Moni below the rule (owner, 24 Nov 2026). Wallets (`wallet-outline`, G W) opens All wallets and Map (`map-marker-outline`, G M) opens Where you spend; each lights on its page and the pages under it, where Home no longer does. The phone tab bar keeps its four tabs and +: the phone layout reaches Wallets from net worth and the wallet cards, and the map from Where you spend, as the phone does.
- **The account tile shows the Google photo** (#143), as the phone's avatar does (`userPhotoUrl` in `@repo/core`), in the sidebar, the rail, the phone layout's Home and the account menu; an email sign-in, or a photo that fails to load, shows the initial. The W2 shell boards draw the initial.
- **Home has one wallets section, the strip** (owner, 24 Nov 2026). Recent, Next 14 days and Where you spend sit in three equal columns under Spent and Review (the rail puts Recent beside the other two, stacked). A layout saved with the old Wallets card drops it. The balance check-in (That's right, Fix it, Not now) sits on All wallets under net worth in landscape, and at the foot of Home on the phone layout, as on the phone.
- **Review is a drawer on Home, not a page** (#104): Home's review card, `?review=` on Home, Ctrl K and G R open it in the slide-over over Home. The sidebar and rail have no Review entry, and old `/review` links redirect to it. Closing a drawer a link opened steps back, so Back after it leaves Home. On the phone layout it is the pushed Review page, as on the phone, with the same words, actions and Recently reviewed.
- **Home's review rows say only the merchant, the amount and when** (#119): the time today, else the day, with Approve as a round check button (a wallet button when the capture needs one). The alert's source and text wait in the drawer. In the drawer's list, the category and the wallet each show with their small bubble, the time sits under the amount, and the toolbar is the count and an Oldest first or Newest first control; nothing is joined with a middle dot. The drawer's capture says how long ago an older one was after its time, as the phone's card does (#130). It shows no place yet: a capture's location lives only on the phone that captured it (`lib/ai/proposal-location-cache.ts`), so the phone's place line (#131) waits on the location being synced with the capture.
- **Phone rules the web bends:** a ⋯ shows only on the hovered or selected row and right-click opens the same menu; a key hint sits inside or touching the control it works (Pace P, the up chevron's K), never in a cluster to one side; an item's detail opens in the slide-over, Moni chat docks on the right and Add slides over, instead of opening as sheets or pushed pages; every passing message (saved, undone, failed, sent) is the one toast, bottom left, and a field shows no Saving or Saved of its own; a sent link's instructions, an offline banner and a filter's empty map stay inline because they hold until something changes; chart tooltips follow the pointer, and a chart shows no legend and no value at rest (#114); desktop pages scroll vertically and never squash a chart to fit the first screen; the seams resize (#95): the slide-over's left edge (up to 240 short of the window) and Moni chat's (up to half the window) and the Activity table's column edges drag wider or narrower, take the arrow keys when focused, reset on a double click or Enter, and are remembered per browser, and a long name in a slide-over's title wraps to two lines before it truncates, with the full text on hover in the table. In landscape (rail and sidebar), Coming up keeps a **Pay** quick action on rows you owe, where the phone and the portrait web show plain rows that open the person (#87). All wallets adds a This month column, what is in your wallets beside what is owed on cards, and Download CSV; a wallet adds Open in Activity and edits its amount in place.
- **Home's wallet strip** (#120, the phone's `XW-WalletCardStyle-Final` cards): one row of wallet cards above the dashboard in landscape and under net worth in portrait, as wide as the page so more cards show, the main currency first, then each other currency behind a divider in its own money ("US$120.00"), then New wallet (which opens New wallet in the slide-over on Wallets). The mouse drags a card within its currency at once (a touch holds 500 ms first, as on the phone), the strip scrolls near its edges, and Alt with ← or → moves the focused card. A drop saves the one order the phone and All wallets share and says "Wallet moved" in the toast with Undo. All wallets on the web has no Reorder mode yet; the strip is its way in. No W2 board draws it yet.
- **An item without a page of its own opens in the slide-over** (#106, after Upwork's Transactions detail): a transaction, a category, a person, Home's Review, and New wallet or Edit wallet. A place is the exception: it swaps into Where you spend's side card, as Google Maps' side panel does, so the map stays live beside it. It exists only while something is open (#102). It slides in from the right on the `sheet` spring over about two thirds of the window (960 wide, always leaving 240 of the page in view), dims the page and makes it inert, and takes the focus. "‹ <page>" at its top left and Esc close it. Opening it pushes the address, so Back closes it and a shared link opens it. Over a list, its top right holds the position and the chevrons, and J and K keep browsing inside it. No surface keeps the docked pane: Activity's J and K work as well inside the slide-over, a budget cell edits in the table without opening it (E edits in it once it is open), and Moni chat is the only pane that docks beside a page. A wallet keeps its own page. On the phone layout the slide-over is the pushed page.
- **Where you spend moves like Google Maps** (owner, 24 Nov 2026): the map is a wide band beside the side card, at most 58% of the window tall and three quarters of its own width (`--map-h`), so Day by day starts on the first screen and the page plainly goes on. A mouse drags it at any zoom and a fling keeps it sliding; the wheel and a trackpad pinch zoom about the pointer, as Google's `greedy` maps do, while the wheel anywhere else scrolls the page; a double click zooms in (Shift out); on touch one finger scrolls the page and two fingers pan and pinch, with "Use two fingers to move the map", as Google's `cooperative` maps do, so a thumb is never trapped. +, − and Fit stay. Picking a pin, a row, J or K swaps the side card for the place (amount, by day, what you bought, payments, Open in Google Maps) on the sheet spring, with "‹ All places" and Esc at its top left and the position and the K and J chevrons at its top right; the map glides only when the pin sits near an edge or inside a merged pin, and stays usable throughout. Back to the list keeps its scroll and filters, and `?place=` keeps Back and a shared link working. In portrait the card, and so the place, stacks under the map, as the phone's card sits under its map.
- **A receipt photo opens full screen.** A transaction with a photo shows it in its slide-over as the phone's row, the photo as the bubble with "Receipt photo" over "Open the slip"; Review's source photo works the same. The photo opens in the phone's viewer, black with the photo contained and × at the top right: it grows out of the thumbnail (a fade under Reduce motion), a click or the wheel zooms, a drag pans, and Esc or a click beside the photo closes it. It loads only through a signed link from the private `receipts` bucket, never a new tab. "Add a receipt photo" stays phone-only: `@repo/data` has no upload for the web yet, so a transaction without a photo shows no row. No board draws it; Activity rows show no receipt mark, as on the phone.
- **Dropdowns, menus and dates are Moni's own.** Never the browser's select or date input. The whole chip or field opens the list, every row spans the menu, and the chosen row is ticked. A popover flips or clamps to stay on screen. Dates are written as the app writes them and picked on Moni's calendar; the Activity Dates filter keeps the phone's presets, and a debt's Due date runs from today to a year ahead with No date, as on the phone (#107). A pane's values share one width, and a value that can't be edited is outlined, not filled.
- **The Ask Moni mark is the cat** everywhere on the web, never the MDI `creation` sparkle.
- **Android and the web only.** No App Store links or iPhone app copy until the owner opens iOS.
- Compliance items designed into the boards: account deletion in the app and on a public page (issue #56), Moni's AI opt-in before any data goes to an AI provider and the AI label on Moni chat (#57), the privacy policy and terms linked from the app and the site (#58), the 18+ age check (#60).

## Open owner decisions
