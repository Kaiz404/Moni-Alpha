## What Moni is, and what it is not {#service}

Moni is a budgeting app. You record your wallets, transactions, budgets, bills and debts, and Moni shows you where your money goes. On Android, Moni can also read payment alerts and receipts to add transactions for you.

Moni is not a bank or a payment service:

- Moni never holds, sends or receives money.
- Moni never connects to your bank account and never asks for your bank password.
- The balances in Moni are worked out from what you record. They can differ from your bank's. Your bank's records are the ones that count.

## No financial advice {#no-advice}

Moni does not give financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Budgets, forecasts, "safe to spend" amounts and answers in Moni chat are estimates from your own records. They can be wrong or out of date. You make your own money decisions. For advice about your situation, ask a licensed professional.

## Moni's AI can be wrong {#ai}

Moni's AI reads payment alerts, receipts and what you type or say, and answers questions in Moni chat. It works only after you turn it on. Moni chat is an AI, not a person.

- AI output can be wrong. It can misread an amount, a date, a shop or a wallet, or miss a payment.
- Moni shows you what the AI read, and you approve it before it becomes a transaction. The exception is a payment that matches what you have filed many times before, or a transfer between your own wallets. Moni adds those itself, marks them, and lets you undo or edit them.
- Check your records. You are responsible for what you approve and keep.

Without the AI, you can still add transactions by hand.
